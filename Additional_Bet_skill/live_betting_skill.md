# Live Betting Skill

## Core Principle
Do not react to score only.

Understand:
- Match flow
- Tempo control
- Transition danger
- Emotional momentum

---

# Key Live Indicators

## Positive Signs
- Sustained pressure
- Dangerous box entries
- Set-piece pressure
- Tactical dominance

## Negative Signs
- Chaotic transitions
- Random long balls
- Emotional loss of structure
- Fatigue collapse

---

# Live Betting Decision Tree

## Enter Trade When:
- Tactical edge visible
- Market overreacts to score
- Underdog cannot sustain pressure
- Favorite creating consistent danger

---

## Avoid Betting When:
- Red card chaos
- End-to-end madness
- Referee instability
- Weather disruption

---

# Psychological Discipline

Never:
- Chase losses
- Bet from frustration
- Double stake impulsively

Good live betting is patience.
