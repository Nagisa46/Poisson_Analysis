# Football Data Checklist

Before betting, check:

## Team News
- Injuries
- Suspensions
- Rotation risk

## Schedule
- Rest days
- Travel fatigue
- European competition

## Tactical
- Formation matchup
- Pressing vulnerability
- Counter attack threat

## Statistics
- xG trend
- Big chances
- Shots inside box
- Set-piece performance

## Market
- Opening odds
- Line movement
- Public bias

## Psychology
- Motivation
- Pressure
- Derby factor

If too many unknowns exist:
NO BET is valid.
