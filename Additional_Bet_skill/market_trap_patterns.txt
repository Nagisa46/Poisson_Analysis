# Market Trap Patterns

## 1. Public Favorite Trap
When:
- Famous club
- Heavy public betting
- Odds become cheaper unexpectedly

Possible meaning:
Sharp money may be opposing public side.

---

## 2. Revenge Narrative Trap
Public overvalues:
- "They lost last time"
- "They want revenge"

Market usually already prices this in.

---

## 3. Fake Momentum Trap
Danger signs:
- Win streak against weak opponents
- Lucky finishing
- Unsustainable xG overperformance

---

## 4. Must-Win Illusion
A must-win team:
- Often becomes overpriced
- Faces defensive low block
- Plays nervously

Motivation alone is not edge.

---

## 5. Early Goal Distortion
After early goal:
- Match state changes completely
- Statistics become distorted

Avoid using raw stats without context.

---

## 6. Derby Chaos
Avoid high exposure on:
- Emotional derby
- Rivalry games
- Matches with unpredictable aggression

Variance becomes very high.

---

## 7. European Competition Fatigue
Teams after UCL/UEL:
- Rotations
- Lower intensity
- Mental fatigue

Especially dangerous away from home.
