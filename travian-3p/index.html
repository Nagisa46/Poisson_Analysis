3 Party — Farming

The way to go for any real farmer who also wants a good base. The 3P strategy leaves more time to profit from your investment because the first party is needed later. It goes for a lot of resource income early and invests in CP afterwards. Getting the CP fixed costs more, but you'll also have more resources to invest, and the base you end up with keeps paying off after settling. It needs a lot of activity and know-how — one slip and it may not pay off.
