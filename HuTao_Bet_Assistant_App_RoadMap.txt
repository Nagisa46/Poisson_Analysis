=====================
3. CURRENT PROS & CONS
=====================

Pros:
- Real betting validation (not theory)
- Multi-league exposure
- Combination of model + human analysis
- Already showing early profitability
- Flexible tool structure

Cons:
- Small sample size (10 bets = high variance)
- No centralized logging system yet
- No league classification (important for edge)
- MY odds not yet standardized to decimal in all cases
- AH / O/U logic still simplified
- No automated decision engine yet

Risks:
- Overfitting early success
- Inconsistent decision criteria
- Market adjustment over time

Data & Logging:

Model Usage:
- Always compare:
  Model Probability vs Market Probability

Odds Handling:
- Convert MY odds to Decimal before EV calculation

Strategy:
- Avoid forcing bets (SKIP is important)
- Use Poisson as baseline, not final decision

Product Direction:
- Move from "calculator" → "decision engine"
- Users want answers, not numbers

Future Build:
- Integrate:
  Poisson + Implied + Live Assistant
- Add auto-highlight value bets
- Build simple dashboard UI

Mindset:
- Think like a system builder, not just bettor
